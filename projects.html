Enveda

Work in progress

Kaggle Competition

Investigating trustworthy molecular identification from tandem mass spectrometry (MS/MS) data using the Enveda CASMI26 competition as a research testbed. The project explores group-conditional confidence calibration, conformal uncertainty estimation, and robustness to spectral changes such as peak dropout and intensity perturbations. The goal is to assess when a predicted molecular structure can be accepted confidently and when further analysis is needed.

Med Guard: A Knowledge-Driven System for Drug Safety Risk Prediction

Personal Project Oct 2025

Med Guard is an explainable AI-driven clinical decision support system that integrates Transformer-based Named Entity Recognition (ClinicalBERT, BioBERT, and spaCy), Neo4j Knowledge Graphs, and Graph RAG to extract medications, diseases, symptoms, dosages, and laboratory findings from unstructured clinical notes. It constructs a domain-specific knowledge graph linking clinical entities, biomedical ontologies, and patient context, while leveraging vector embeddings, semantic retrieval, and graph traversal to retrieve relevant evidence from clinical guidelines and medical literature. The system performs drug–drug, drug–disease, and contraindication risk analysis, grounding LLM responses with retrieved evidence to reduce hallucinations and generate transparent, evidence-backed safety recommendations with explainable reasoning for clinicians.

U.S. Patent Phrase to Phrase Matching

(Kaggle Silver Medal Winner)

Kaggle Competition Jun 2022

Developed a semantic similarity model to identify key phrases and contextual relationships within patent documents. Leveraged transformer based architectures and domain context features to accurately match semantically similar phrases across diverse technical domains. The solution followed a CV inspired strategy with customized transformer heads and effective preprocessing which significantly improved contextual understanding and overall performance. This approach supports efficient patent examination by improving retrieval and reducing redundancy in prior art searches.

NBME - Score Clinical Patient Notes

(Kaggle Bronze Medal Winner)

Kaggle Competition May 2022

Developed an NLP solution to automatically identify key clinical concepts in medical student patient notes, mapping rubric features (e.g., “diminished appetite”) to diverse textual expressions (e.g., “eating less”). I used transformer-based models with customized heads and a cross-validation strategy to ensure accurate and reliable detection of clinical features. This solution improves scoring efficiency, enhances interpretability, and helps medical educators provide timely, actionable feedback.
